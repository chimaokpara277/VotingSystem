# VotingSystem

A decentralized governance platform that incentivizes participation in community decision-making.

## Overview

VotingSystem is a blockchain-based platform that enables:
- Community members to register their voting power
- Incentivized participation in governance decisions
- Fair distribution of rewards based on voting activity

## Features

- **Vote Registration**: Record and track voting power
- **Participation Incentives**: Automatic incentive distribution to encourage voting
- **Proportional Influence**: Voting power proportional to participation level
- **Governance Controls**: Administrative oversight for system integrity

## Functions

- `launch`: Initialize the system with a governance administrator
- `register-votes`: Record voting power for governance decisions
- `distribute-incentives`: Calculate and distribute participation incentives
- `execute-vote`: Allow voters to execute their votes and claim incentives

## Getting Started

1. Deploy the contract to your blockchain
2. Launch the system with a trusted governance administrator
3. Community members can begin registering their voting power
4. Periodically distribute incentives to encourage ongoing participation

## Security

The system includes permission controls to ensure only authorized administrators can perform sensitive operations like incentive distribution.
